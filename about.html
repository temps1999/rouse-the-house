WHY I STARTED ROUSE THE HOUSE

Cleaning should be done properly.

I've spent years working in both commercial and domestic cleaning, including working for larger cleaning companies.

Over that time, I've seen how easy it can become for cleaning to be focused on one thing: getting the job finished as quickly as possible.

When you're working to tight schedules and targets, corners can sometimes be cut just to get through the workload. That's something I've never really been comfortable with, because I take pride in the work I do.

That's one of the reasons I decided to start Rouse the House.

I wanted to create a cleaning service where I can take the time to do things properly, pay attention to the details and leave a home knowing I've genuinely made a difference — rather than simply ticking a job off a list.

With years of experience across both domestic and commercial cleaning, I understand that every property is different. Whether it's a regular clean, a deep clean, an end-of-tenancy clean or keeping a business premises looking its best, I'll work around what you actually need.
