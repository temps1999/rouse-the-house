A little about us

Good work.
Done with care.

Rouse the House is an independent cleaning business based in Ramsgate, serving homes, landlords and businesses across Thanet and surrounding areas. The aim is simple: dependable work, thoughtful details and a personal service from first message to finished clean.

Local roots. Family at heart.

A local face.
A personal promise.

Hi, I’m Conor. I’ve lived in Ramsgate my whole life, so this isn’t just an area on a map to me, it’s home. I know how much local people, families and small businesses matter to the community, and I want Rouse the House to be a business that people around Thanet feel comfortable calling on.

I started this business because I want to build something better for myself and, more importantly, create a stronger foundation for my family and their future. I want to put the work in now, grow something I can be proud of, and earn people’s trust one job at a time.

That’s why every clean matters to me, no matter how big or small the job might seem. Whether it’s a single oven, a fridge that needs some attention, a full deep clean or regular work for a local business, I’ll give it my best and take care with the details. I never want a customer to feel like their job wasn’t important, because to me, doing a job properly is part of keeping the promise I’ve made to my customers and my family.

I’m not here to make big promises and disappear. I want to be friendly, honest and dependable, to listen to what you need, explain things clearly and leave you feeling happy you chose me. When you book Rouse the House, you’re supporting a local family business, and I’ll do my best to make sure that trust is well placed.

Conor, Founder of Rouse the House Cleaning Services
