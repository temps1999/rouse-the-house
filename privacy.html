How information shared with Rouse the House Cleaning Services may be used when you contact us.

Last updated: October 2026

Information you choose to share

If you contact us by email, telephone or WhatsApp, we may use the details you provide to respond to your enquiry, discuss cleaning requirements and provide a quote. Please avoid sending information that is not needed for your enquiry.

How we use it

Contact details and enquiry information are used to respond to you and, where applicable, arrange cleaning services. We do not sell your personal information. We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry, service administration or applicable legal obligations.

Third-party services

Links to WhatsApp and your email app are handled by those providers under their own terms and privacy policies. This website’s quote form currently prepares an email draft in your device’s email app; it does not transmit form data to a website database.
